Clinical Excellence at Home

Recovery should never be compromised by the difficulty of travelling.

Navigating city traffic, climbing staircases, and enduring long car rides immediately after joint replacement surgery or following a neurological event can aggravate acute pain and increase injury risk.

Aarav Clinics provides comprehensive Home Physiotherapy services across Hyderabad. Our licensed physiotherapists travel directly to your doorstep equipped with portable electrotherapy units, ultrasound modalities, resistance training bands, balance cushions, and measurement tools.

Who Benefits Most

Ideal candidates for In-Home Physiotherapy

Post-Surgical Joint Replacement

Immediate phase recovery following Total Knee Replacement (TKR) or Total Hip Replacement (THR).

Neurological Recovery

Bedbound hemiparetic patients, transfer retraining, spasticity management, and home obstacle navigation.

Geriatric Frailty

Elderly family members with general muscle deconditioning, Parkinson's disease, or fear of falling.

Severe Acute Pain

Acute lumbar disc bulges with incapacitating spasms preventing standing or sitting in a car.

Booking Process

How our in-home therapy visits work

01

Initial Case Review

We review clinical history to assign the ideal specialized physiotherapist and equip them appropriately.

02

Bedside Assessment

Evaluating joint range, swelling, vital signs, muscle strength, bed mobility, and home environment fall risks.

03

Therapeutic Session

Delivering pain-relieving electrotherapy, manual mobilization, gentle passive stretching, and safe standing drills.

04

Caregiver Guidance

Training family members on safe transfer mechanics, position changes, and daily exercise compliance.
