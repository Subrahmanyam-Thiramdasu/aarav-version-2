Overview

Root-cause physical rehabilitation, not temporary fixes.

Physiotherapy at Aarav Clinics goes beyond superficial heating pads or generic exercise printouts. We analyze joint biomechanics, neural tension, muscular imbalances, and daily ergonomic habits to create a structured recovery protocol.

Whether you are suffering from acute disc herniation, frozen shoulder, chronic knee osteoarthritis, or desk-induced postural strain, our goal is lasting functional relief. By pairing hands-on manual techniques with guided strengthening, we eliminate pain while protecting against future re-injury.

Conditions Treated

Common concerns we resolve daily

Lower Back Pain & Sciatica

Herniated discs, lumbar spondylosis, piriformis syndrome, and radiating nerve pain.

Neck Pain & Cervical Spondylosis

Tech neck stiffness, cervical nerve compression, and posture-induced tension headaches.

Frozen Shoulder & Impingement

Adhesive capsulitis, rotator cuff tendinitis, and painful overhead movement restrictions.

Knee & Joint Osteoarthritis

Cartilage wear, joint stiffness, patellofemoral pain, and weight-bearing discomfort.

Our Methodology

A 4-phase clinical rehabilitation pathway

01

Thorough Assessment

Postural mapping, range of motion measurements, and joint palpatory testing to pinpoint tissue origin.

02

Hands-On Manual Therapy

Joint mobilization, myofascial release, and passive stretching to release spasm and restore joint play.

03

Advanced Modalities

Class IV therapeutic laser, interferential therapy (IFT), and ultrasound for deep cellular inflammation control.

04

Active Strengthening

Customized progressive load resistance exercises stabilizing deep postural stabilizers to prevent relapse.

Your First Visit

What to expect during evaluation

1

Clinical Interview & History Review

We review prior scans (MRI, X-Rays), discuss symptom triggers, pain timeline, and daily functional limitations.

2

Physical Examination & Testing

Detailed evaluation of joint mobility, nerve conduction tension tests, gait analysis, and strength balances.

3

Initial Relief Treatment

We do not make you wait; your first session includes immediate manual decompression and pain-relieving therapy.

Measurable Outcomes You Can Expect

  • Rapid relief from acute radiating nerve pain
  • Restoration of full joint range of motion
  • Reduction in dependence on painkiller medications
  • Safe return to desk work, driving, and fitness routines
