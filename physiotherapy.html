Our Approach

Root-cause physical rehabilitation, not temporary fixes.

Physiotherapy at Aarav Clinics goes beyond superficial heating pads or generic exercise printouts. We analyze joint biomechanics, neural tension, muscular imbalances, and daily ergonomic habits to create a structured recovery protocol.

Whether you are suffering from acute disc herniation, frozen shoulder, chronic knee osteoarthritis, or desk-induced postural strain, our goal is lasting functional relief. By pairing hands-on manual techniques with guided strengthening, we eliminate pain while protecting against future re-injury.

Our Methodology

A 4-phase clinical rehabilitation pathway

Structured for precise assessment, immediate relief, and long-term functional restoration.

1. Thorough Assessment

Postural mapping, range of motion measurements, and joint palpatory testing to pinpoint tissue origin.

2. Manual Therapy

Joint mobilization, myofascial release, and passive stretching to release spasm and restore joint play.

3. Advanced Modalities

Class IV therapeutic laser, interferential therapy (IFT), and ultrasound for deep cellular inflammation control.

4. Active Strengthening

Customized progressive load resistance exercises stabilizing deep postural stabilizers to prevent relapse.
