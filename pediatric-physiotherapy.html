Pediatric Physiotherapy in Hyderabad

Empowering infants and children to achieve critical gross motor milestones—rolling, sitting, crawling, and walking—through joyful, play-based physical rehabilitation.

Playful, Milestone-Centered Therapy

Every child develops at their own pace, but early support creates lasting leaps.

Movement is how children explore the world, build neural pathways, and gain confidence. When a child experiences low muscle tone, tightness, or delayed motor milestones, gentle intervention helps guide their body toward natural movement patterns.

At Aarav Clinics Child Development Centre, our pediatric physiotherapists work in a dedicated, warmly equipped pediatric gym featuring soft-play mats, therapy balls, balance beams, and climbing wedges. We disguise rigorous developmental therapy inside games, sensory obstacle courses, and play, so children look forward to every session while gaining measurable physical strength.

Conditions We Address

Pediatric motor and orthopedic concerns

Gross Motor Delays

Babies and toddlers delayed in rolling over, sitting unassisted, crawling, or independent walking.

Cerebral Palsy (CP)

Spasticity management, functional mobility training, postural positioning, and contracture prevention.

Torticollis & Plagiocephaly

Infant head tilt preference, sternocleidomastoid tightness, and positional head flattening.

Hypotonia & Joint Laxity

"Floppy" infant syndrome, weak trunk stability, frequent falls, toe walking, and flat feet.

Therapeutic Methodology

How we guide your child's physical development

01

Standardized Motor Assessment

Evaluating age-normed developmental scales (GMFM, PDMS-2), muscle tone, joint ranges, and primitive reflex integration.

02

Play-Based Neurodevelopmental Therapy (NDT)

Facilitating natural righting and equilibrium reactions using therapeutic Swiss balls, bolster swings, and wedges.

03

Gait & Balance Retraining

Correcting toe-walking habits, strengthening foot arches, improving stride symmetry, and balance obstacle courses.

04

Home Play Coaching for Parents

Equipping parents with fun positioning techniques, tummy time routines, and daily play games to practice at home.

Key Milestones Achieved Through Pediatric Therapy

  • Independent Mobility: Gaining trunk stability and leg power to sit, stand, and walk with confidence.
  • Correct Posture & Alignment: Relieving infant torticollis and correcting scoliosis or gait deviations early.
  • Enhanced Coordination & Stamina: Building stamina so children can run, jump, and play alongside peers effortlessly.
  • Emotional Confidence: Removing movement anxiety and watching your child celebrate every new physical milestone.
