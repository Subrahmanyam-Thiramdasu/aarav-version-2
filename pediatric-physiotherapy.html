Child Development Centre

Strength in Motion. Joy in Play.

Empowering infants and children to achieve critical gross motor milestones—rolling, sitting, crawling, and walking—through purposeful, joyful physical rehabilitation in our sensory-rich clinical gym.

Book Developmental Evaluation
Playful, Milestone-Centered Therapy

Every child develops at their own pace, but early support creates lasting leaps.

Movement is how children explore the world, build neural pathways, and gain confidence. When a child experiences low muscle tone, tightness, or delayed motor milestones, gentle intervention helps guide their body toward natural movement patterns.

At Aarav Clinics Child Development Centre, our pediatric physiotherapists work in a dedicated, warmly equipped pediatric gym featuring soft-play mats, therapy balls, balance beams, and climbing wedges. We disguise rigorous developmental therapy inside games, sensory obstacle courses, and play, so children look forward to every session while gaining measurable physical strength.

Pediatric motor and orthopedic concerns we address

Gross Motor Delays

Assistance for babies who are late to roll over, sit independently, pull to stand, or take their first steps.

Muscle Tone Disorders

Therapy for hypotonia (floppy infant syndrome) or hypertonia (stiffness), often associated with Cerebral Palsy or genetic conditions.

Torticollis & Plagiocephaly

Gentle neck stretching and repositioning strategies for infants with a head tilt or flat spot on the skull.

Gait Abnormalities

Correction of toe walking, in-toeing (pigeon toes), out-toeing, and frequent tripping or clumsiness.
