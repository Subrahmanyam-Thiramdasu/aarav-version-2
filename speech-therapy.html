Unlocking the Power of Voice

Communication is fundamental to human connection, learning, and self-esteem.

Whether a child is struggling to speak their first words, a preschooler is frustrated because others cannot understand them, or an adult is managing stammering or post-stroke aphasia, speech therapy opens the door to fluent communication.

At Aarav Clinics, our Speech-Language Pathology department is led by Dr. Karthik Varma (MASLP). We combine comprehensive standardized acoustic assessments with engaging, child-centered therapeutic play. We target oral motor coordination, vocal inflection, receptive comprehension, sentence formulation, and social pragmatics in a warm, low-pressure environment.

Communication Difficulties

Speech and language conditions we treat

Toddler Speech & Language Delays

Limited vocabulary, absence of 2-word combinations by age 2, and difficulty following simple instructions.

Articulation & Clarity Issues

Sound substitutions, lisps, omissions, or tongue thrust habits that make speech difficult to understand.

Stuttering & Stammering (Fluency)

Repetitions, prolonged syllable blocks, physical tension while speaking, and performance speaking anxiety.

Social Pragmatics & Autism Speech

Difficulty making eye contact, conversational turn-taking, echolalia, and understanding non-verbal cues.

Our Clinical Methodology

Structured speech-language therapy approach

01

Formal Speech & Language Profiling

Standardized diagnostic evaluation assessing phonology, oral motor anatomy, receptive-expressive language index, and fluency baseline.

02

Oral Placement Therapy (OPT)

Targeted exercises for jaw stability, tongue elevation, and lip closure using specialized therapy tools to build articulatory muscle strength.

03

Naturalistic Play Interaction

Embedding phonetic practice into interactive play, storytelling, and songs to foster spontaneous expressive language.

04

Parent Communication Coaching

Training parents on wait-time techniques, linguistic modeling, conversational expansion, and screen-time management at home.

Transformative Outcomes of Speech Therapy

  • Clear, Intelligible Pronunciation: Allowing family, teachers, and classmates to understand your child with ease.
  • Reduced Behavioral Frustration: Eliminating tantrums triggered by an inability to express wants, needs, and emotions.
  • Fluent Conversational Rhythm: Mastering smooth breathing patterns and speech rhythm to overcome stammering blocks.
  • Classroom & Academic Readiness: Building phonemic awareness, vocabulary depth, and storytelling skills essential for school success.
