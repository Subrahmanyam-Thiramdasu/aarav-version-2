Speech-Language Pathology

Unlocking the Power of Voice.

Nurturing speech clarity, expressive vocabulary, fluent communication, and social confidence under the guidance of licensed speech pathologists led by Dr. Karthik Varma (MASLP).

Book Speech Assessment
Expressive Connection

Communication is fundamental to human connection, learning, and self-esteem.

Whether a child is struggling to speak their first words, a preschooler is frustrated because others cannot understand them, or an adult is managing stammering or post-stroke aphasia, speech therapy opens the door to fluent communication.

At Aarav Clinics, our Speech-Language Pathology department combines comprehensive standardized acoustic assessments with engaging, child-centered therapeutic play. We target oral motor coordination, vocal inflection, receptive comprehension, sentence formulation, and social pragmatics in a warm, low-pressure environment.

Speech and language conditions we treat

Articulation & Phonological Disorders

Helping children pronounce sounds correctly so they can be easily understood by peers and teachers.

Language Delays

Expanding vocabulary, improving sentence structure, and enhancing comprehension of instructions (Receptive & Expressive Language).

Stuttering & Fluency

Techniques for smooth speech flow, reducing hesitations, blocks, and prolongations in children and adults.

Social (Pragmatic) Communication

Teaching children how to use language appropriately in social situations, take turns, and read non-verbal cues (often for ASD).

Our Therapy Approach

  • Play-Based Modeling: Engaging the child through their favorite toys to naturally elicit sounds and words.
  • PROMPT Therapy principles: Tactile cues on the face and jaw to guide correct oral motor movements for articulation.
  • Alternative & Augmentative Communication (AAC): Introducing visual boards or devices for non-verbal children.
  • Parent Training: Equipping families with exact language stimulation strategies (like narrating, pausing, and expanding) to use during daily routines.
