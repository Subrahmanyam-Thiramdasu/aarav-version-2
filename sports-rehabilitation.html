Sport-Specific Recovery

Peak athletic performance without fear of relapse.

An athletic injury is more than just damaged tissue—it impacts muscle firing patterns, proprioceptive joint balance, and athletic confidence.

At Aarav Clinics, our sports rehabilitation programs bridge the critical gap between "pain-free daily walking" and "full competition speed." We reconstruct the kinetic chain through progressive loading, neuromuscular re-education, and sport-specific movement simulations.

Injuries We Treat

From sprains to complex ligament tears

ACL & Meniscus Tears

Pre-operative prehab and structured post-operative return-to-sport protocols.

Shoulder Sprains

Throwing injuries, impingement, and labral stabilization for overhead athletes.

Hamstring Strains

High-speed running injuries, eccentric lengthening repair, and scar tissue mobilization.

Achilles Tendonitis

Inversion trauma, chronic instability, and high-load tendon rebuilding.

The Athlete's Roadmap

Phased return-to-play protocol

1. Acute Protection

Tissue swelling management, gentle joint drainage, and laser photobiomodulation.

2. Restoring Mobility

Gentle manual therapy, active range recovery, and joint arthrokinematics restoration.

3. Progressive Loading

Heavy slow resistance training and eccentric muscle loading to rebuild tendon elasticity.

4. Sport-Specific Agility

Plyometrics, deceleration mechanics, directional cutting, and competition clearance.
