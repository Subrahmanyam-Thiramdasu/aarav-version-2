Professional Biography

Dr. Ananya Iyer is a compassionate Pediatric and Occupational Therapist dedicated to helping children overcome developmental hurdles. She believes that play is the language of childhood, using interactive and sensory-rich methods to build foundational skills for daily life.

From guiding neurodivergent children toward confident self-regulation to assisting with foundational fine motor activities like handwriting and dressing, Dr. Ananya's approach is family-centered. She equips parents with practical strategies, ensuring therapeutic progress extends well beyond the clinic walls.
