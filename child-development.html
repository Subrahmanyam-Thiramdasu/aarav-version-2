Our Philosophy

Why early intervention shapes a lifetime.

The first five years of life represent an unprecedented period of neuroplasticity. When developmental delays, speech hesitations, or sensory difficulties are identified and addressed early, children adapt with remarkable speed and confidence.

At Aarav Clinics, therapy never feels intimidating or sterile. We use play-based, scientifically validated protocols where your child builds essential life skills through purposeful, joyful activities.

Therapy Specialties

Coordinated services for your child's growth

Every child is unique. Our clinical team tailors care plans that combine any of the following disciplines.

Pediatric Physiotherapy

Assisting children who struggle with rolling, sitting, crawling, or walking. Our therapists manage gross motor delays, cerebral palsy, hypotonia (low muscle tone), toe walking, torticollis, and balance challenges with compassionate physical therapy.

Explore Pediatric Physio →

Speech & Language Therapy

Helping toddlers and children find their voice. From first words and vocabulary building to articulation clarity, stammering/fluency support, social pragmatic language, and oral-motor feeding therapy guided by licensed speech-language pathologists.

Explore Speech Therapy →

Occupational Therapy & Sensory Integration

Equipping children for school and daily life. We address sensory processing sensitivities (sound, touch, textures), fine motor dexterity (pencil grip, cutting), hand-eye coordination, emotional regulation, and attention span.

Explore Occupational Therapy →

Autism & Early Intervention Therapy

Individualized developmental and behavioral programs for children on the autism spectrum. We foster mutual communication, social engagement, routine transitions, and sensory calming strategies in close partnership with parents.
