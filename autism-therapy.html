Understanding the Spectrum

Every autistic child possesses unique strengths, perspectives, and potential.

Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) is a neurological divergence — not a disease. At Aarav Clinics, our program is built around each child's individuality. We unite speech pathologists, occupational therapists, and physiotherapists to help children regulate sensory overwhelm, build functional communication, and thrive in daily school and family life.

Speech Therapy

Expanding expressive language, AAC communication, echolalia modulation, and conversational turn-taking through play.

Sensory Regulation

Occupational therapy for sensory processing, vestibular integration, and meltdown prevention using calming gym activities.

Social & Behavioral

Child-led floor time, joint attention, peer parallel play, and positive behavioral reinforcement strategies for families.

Areas of Intervention

Core challenges addressed in our Autism program

Functional Communication & Speech

Expanding expressive language, alternative communication (PECS/AAC), echolalia modulation, and conversational turn-taking.

Sensory Overload & Regulation

De-escalating sensory meltdowns triggered by loud noise, tactile defensiveness, bright lighting, or crowd stimulation.

Social Engagement & Joint Attention

Encouraging shared eye gaze, interactive floor-time games, empathy development, and peer parallel play.

Rigid Routines & Transition Anxiety

Easing distress when shifting between activities, leaving home, trying unfamiliar foods, or adapting to new school schedules.

Eye Contact & Imitation

Building reciprocal gaze, social referencing, gestural imitation, and shared enjoyment in play through natural learning environments.

Feeding & Mealtime Sensory Aversions

Addressing selective eating, texture aversions, and mealtime rituals through gradual sensory exposure and behavioral strategies.

Comprehensive Protocol

Our integrated 4-phase Autism intervention model

We begin with understanding your child — not with rigid protocols. Each phase is adapted to the child's current developmental level and the family's priorities.

01

Multidisciplinary Baseline Mapping

Collaborative assessment by our speech pathologist, occupational therapist, and pediatric physiotherapist evaluating sensory profiles and developmental scores.

02

Sensory Diet & Calming Environment

Creating individualized vestibular and proprioceptive schedules to stabilize the child's nervous system and eliminate fight-or-flight panic through calm sensory gym activities.

03

Child-Led Relationship Development

Utilizing natural interest motifs — trains, numbers, music, animals — to engage the child in reciprocal verbal and gestural interactions at their own pace.

04

Parent Empowerment & Home Strategies

Guiding parents with positive behavioral reinforcement techniques, visual schedule boards, AAC introduction, and mealtime sensory adaptations for home and school.

Lasting Growth

What families experience over time

Spontaneous Expression

Moving from tantrums to expressing hunger, thirst, preferences, and emotions clearly — verbally or through AAC.

Drastically Reduced Meltdowns

Sensory calming strategies that prevent sensory overload before it erupts into full meltdowns at home or school.

Joyful Family Connections

Meaningful joint play, warm reciprocal smiles, and comfortable family outings that feel possible again.

School & Classroom Integration

Sitting comfortably, understanding visual prompts, and interacting gently with peers in everyday classroom settings.

Our Guiding Principles for Autism Support

  • Neurodiversity is not a deficit. We celebrate how each child processes and experiences the world.
  • No diagnosis needed to start. We begin supportive therapy while families pursue medical consultations.
  • Calm environments always. Our clinic is designed for sensory safety — soft lighting, quiet zones, and minimal overwhelm.
  • Family as co-therapists. Parents are trained and empowered to extend therapy outcomes at home every day.
  • Child-paced, never forced. We follow the child's interests and comfort — therapy never feels punishing.
  • Integrated under one roof. No multi-clinic travel — speech, OT, and physio coordinate daily on one plan.
