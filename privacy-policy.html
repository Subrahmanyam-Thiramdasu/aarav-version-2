Patient Privacy Policy

Your medical history, developmental records, and personal contact details are held in strictest medical confidentiality at Aarav Clinics.

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Aarav Clinics ("we", "us", or "our") operates the clinical facility located at HIG, 2nd Floor, Sai Sadan Enclave, H.No. 23/82/2, Plot No. 22 & 3, Sy No. 203, Opposite Spashta Eye Hospital, Near Ramachandrapuram, Jyothi Nagar, Ashok Nagar, Hyderabad, Telangana 502032. We provide physical rehabilitation, speech therapy, occupational therapy, and child development services.

This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, store, utilize, and protect your information when you browse our website, book an appointment, or undergo clinical therapy with us.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect data necessary to coordinate appointments and provide safe, effective clinical therapy:

  • Contact Details: Your name, phone number, email address, and home locality for appointment scheduling and home therapy visits.
  • Clinical & Health Information: Symptoms, medical diagnoses, surgical history, developmental milestone observations, and therapist progress notes provided during assessment.
  • Website Interaction Data: Non-personally identifiable technical analytics (browser type, device resolution, pages viewed) to optimize user experience.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for medical and operational purposes:

  • Scheduling clinic consultations, home physiotherapy visits, and video therapy sessions.
  • Formulating and updating individualized clinical rehabilitation or child development plans.
  • Direct communication with patients and parents regarding appointment timings, exercise programs, or session confirmations over WhatsApp and phone.
  • Complying with medical record-keeping standards and applicable Indian healthcare laws.

4. WhatsApp & Third-Party Communication

When you submit an appointment request on our website, your name, telephone number, and requested service are formatted into an encrypted WhatsApp message sent to our verified clinic number (+91 63028 82910). We never sell, rent, or trade your contact details with third-party marketing companies.

5. Confidentiality of Pediatric Records

We take exceptional care with all developmental, psychological, and behavioral observations concerning minors. Pediatric assessments, speech milestones, and occupational therapy logs are accessible solely to the treating therapist and the child's parents or legal guardians.

6. Data Security & Retention

We employ industry-standard digital security and strict physical access controls at our clinic premises to safeguard patient records against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. Clinical records are retained in compliance with statutory medical council guidelines.
