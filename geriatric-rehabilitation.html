Geriatric Rehabilitation. Safe, Confident Senior Mobility.

Compassionate, evidence-based senior physical therapy focused on balance stabilization, fall prevention, joint flexibility, bone health, and maintaining daily independence.

Healthy Active Aging

Preserving autonomy, strength, and confidence in the golden years.

Aging should not mean accepting a loss of independence, constant joint pain, or the perpetual fear of falling.

Geriatric Rehabilitation at Aarav Clinics is tailored to the physiological changes of senior adults. We address age-related sarcopenia (muscle loss), joint stiffness from osteoarthritis, spinal stenosis, and vestibular balance decline. Our clinicians design gentle, dignified exercise programs that enhance bone density, improve gait stability, prevent dangerous slips and falls, and keep senior citizens actively engaged in the activities they love.

Clinical Indications

Who benefits most from Geriatric Rehabilitation

Tailored clinical protocols addressing acute limitations, chronic restrictions, and functional rehabilitation goals.

Balance Insecurity & Fall Prevention

Unsteadiness while walking, dizziness when turning, or recovering from a previous frightening fall.

Degenerative Osteoarthritis & Stiffness

Severe joint pain and stiffness in the knees, hips, and spine making standing and stair climbing painful.

Osteopenia & Osteoporosis Support

Safe bone-loading and postural exercises designed to reduce vertebral compression fracture risks.

Post-Hospitalization Deconditioning

Generalized weakness and stamina loss following medical illness, surgery, or prolonged bed rest.

Clinical Pathway

How the treatment process works

Structured for meticulous assessment, comfortable delivery, and long-term functional stability.

1. Fall Risk & Mobility Audit

Comprehensive screening of balance, gait speed, joint flexibility, vision, and footwear safety.

2. Joint Mobilization & Pain Relief

Gentle manual therapy, warmth, and safe modalities to ease arthritic morning stiffness.

3. Static & Dynamic Balance Training

Weight-shifting exercises, tandem standing, and obstacle stepping supported by sturdy safety rails.

4. Home Safety & Assistive Advice

Guidance on cane/walker sizing, bathroom grab bars, non-slip flooring, and everyday ergonomic safety.

Treatment Outcomes

Measurable goals you can expect.

Our evidence-based protocols are designed around concrete functional milestones—helping you move easier, build stability, and restore independence.

01

Significant Fall Risk Reduction

Strengthens ankle proprioception and core postural reflexes to prevent disastrous slips and trips.

02

Long-Term Living Autonomy

Maintains ability to stand up from chairs, walk to the market, and manage self-care without relying on others.

03

Safe Cardiovascular Stamina

Rebuilds cardiopulmonary endurance through low-impact, supervised physical conditioning.

What to Expect in Your Consultation

We welcome senior patients with warmth, respect, and patience. The initial consultation moves at a comfortable pace, checking vital signs, reviewing medication lists, and identifying personal mobility goals without physical strain.

Clinical Precautions & Suitability

Safety is paramount. Exercises are conducted with close therapist contact and support rails. Vital signs (blood pressure, pulse oximetry) are checked when indicated, and exercises avoid sudden rotational strain on osteoporotic spines.
