Neurological Physiotherapy

Every Step Forward. Reclaiming Independence.

Rebuilding neural pathways, restoring movement symmetry, retraining gait balance, and reclaiming independence with certified neuro-rehabilitation specialists.

Book Clinical Assessment
Harnessing Neuroplasticity

The human brain has an extraordinary capacity to rewire and recover.

A stroke disrupts neural signalling between the brain and muscular system. However, through structured, task-specific, high-repetition physiotherapy, undamaged brain regions can form new synaptic pathways to restore motor function.

At Aarav Clinics, our stroke rehabilitation program is led by experienced neuro-physiotherapists who understand that recovery does not stop at hospital discharge. Whether you are in the acute post-stroke window or managing chronic impairments years later, our focused protocols help rebuild strength, posture, and self-reliance.

Deficits Addressed

Targeted neurological symptoms we treat

Hemiparesis

Unilateral weakness or paralysis affecting the upper limb, trunk, or leg on one side of the body.

Spasticity

Involuntary muscle tightness and hypertonia that restrict joint mobility and cause painful contractures.

Gait Impairment

Asymmetrical walking patterns, circumduction gait, foot drop, and heightened fear of falls.

Coordination Loss

Loss of fine motor control, hand grip weakness, and difficulty manipulating eating utensils.

Our Methodology

Evidence-based neurological rehabilitation

1. Motor Practice

Repetitive, goal-directed actions such as reaching, grasping, and stepping to forge new neurological wiring.

2. Tone Modulation

PNF stretching, manual neuromuscular release, and therapeutic splint guidance to suppress abnormal synergy patterns.

3. Balance Training

Parallel bar training, body-weight supported step cycles, obstacle stepping, and sensory perturbation.

4. ADL Independence

Training family caregivers and patients in bed mobility, safe transfer techniques, and adaptive self-care methods.
