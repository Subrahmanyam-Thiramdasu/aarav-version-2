Specialised Medical, Rehabilitation & Therapy Services

Aarav Clinics brings together multidisciplinary physical therapy, cutting-edge pain relief technologies, and comprehensive developmental pediatric therapies under one roof in Ashok Nagar, Hyderabad. Every treatment plan is tailored by clinical specialists using proven protocols to restore mobility, alleviate pain, accelerate tissue healing, and support lasting health.

Advanced Technology

Shockwave Therapy

Acoustic pressure waves stimulating cellular repair, microcirculation, and breakdown of stubborn calcifications for chronic plantar fasciitis and tendinopathy.

Learn More Advanced Technology

Advanced Decompression Therapy

Computerized non-surgical spinal decompression gently relieving intervertebral disc pressure, sciatica, herniated discs, and persistent cervical radiculopathy.

Learn More Advanced Technology

Cage Therapy

Structured suspension and universal exercise cage therapy providing gravity-eliminated neuromuscular re-education, postural alignment, and balance stability.

Learn More Advanced Technology

Cupping Therapy

Medical-grade myofascial cupping utilizing controlled decompression to improve localized blood flow, release fascial restrictions, and alleviate dense muscle stiffness.

Dry Needling Therapy

Targeted fine-filament intramuscular needling releasing hyperirritable myofascial trigger points, resetting neuromuscular tension, and relieving referred pain.
