Collaborative Care

The power of a synchronized clinical team.

In traditional setups, a child with developmental delays or an adult recovering from a stroke sees independent therapists who rarely communicate. This creates fragmented progress and conflicting exercise recommendations.

At Aarav Clinics, our physiotherapists, speech therapists, and occupational therapists coordinate directly:

  • Shared diagnostic assessments
  • Unified milestone goal planning
  • Harmonized home exercise instructions
  • One point of contact for family updates
