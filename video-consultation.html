Expert Therapy, No Boundaries

Access senior clinical guidance without geographical limits.

Busy schedules, travel constraints, living overseas — none of these should keep you from gold-standard clinical advice. Our tele-rehabilitation platform provides live visual assessment, posture analysis, and customized rehab programs through a one-click, secure video call.

Ideal Consultation Scenarios

What can be addressed effectively over video

Workstation Ergonomics

Real-time assessment of chair height, monitor distance, neck angle, and lumbar support to eliminate desk-related pain.

MRI, CT & X-Ray Reviews

In-depth clinical interpretation of imaging reports — disc bulges, meniscus tears, or surgery decisions explained clearly.

Pediatric Milestone Screening

Guidance on toddler speech development, walking delays, or sensory concerns using parent-captured video clips.

Exercise Form Correction

Live visual feedback ensuring home rehab routines and strengthening drills are executed with safe biomechanics.
