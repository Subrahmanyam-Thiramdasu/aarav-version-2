Where Clinical Excellence Meets Compassionate Care.

Located in Ashok Nagar, Hyderabad, Aarav Clinics brings together specialized physiotherapy, neurological rehabilitation, and comprehensive child development therapies under a single, highly integrated roof.

Too often, patients and parents find themselves shuttling between isolated clinics for physical rehabilitation, speech therapy, and occupational support. We built Aarav Clinics as a sanctuary where multidisciplinary specialists collaborate directly on each patient's tailored care plan.
